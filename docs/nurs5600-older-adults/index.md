# 👵 NURS5600 — Nursing of Older Adults

Gerontological nursing = the art of seeing the **person**, not just the age. 🌟 Hong Kong's population is greying fast 📈 — this is arguably the most future-proof specialty of all.

!!! abstract "📖 What this course is about"
    Normal ageing vs. disease 🧓, comprehensive geriatric assessment, polypharmacy 💊, falls 🍌, dementia care 🧩, frailty, and promoting dignity & independence in every setting — hospital, community, and residential care 🏡.

!!! warning "⚠️ Mindset check"
    Ageism is a clinical risk ⚠️ — "it's just old age" is how treatable problems get missed. Stay curious! 🔍

## 📋 Topics to Come

- 🧓 Theories of ageing & age-friendly care
- 📋 Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment (CGA)
- 💊 Polypharmacy & deprescribing principles
- 🍌 Falls prevention & post-fall management
- 🧩 Dementia, delirium & depression (the 3 D's)
- 🏡 Community & residential care models
- ⚖️ Elder abuse recognition & ethics

## 🧭 The 4Ms of Age-Friendly Care

- 🧠 **What Matters** — goals & preferences
- 💊 **Medication** — right drug, right dose, right elder
- 🧩 **Mentation** — mind & mood
- 🚶 **Mobility** — move safely, every day

!!! tip "💡 Exam pearl"
    Delirium vs dementia vs depression questions **always** show up. Make a comparison table early! 📊
