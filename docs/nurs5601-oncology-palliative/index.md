# 🎗️ NURS5601 — Oncology Nursing and Palliative Care

The course where science 🔬 meets heart ❤️. Oncology nursing asks for sharp clinical skills; palliative care asks for presence, honesty, and comfort-first thinking 🕊️.

!!! abstract "📖 What this course is about"
    From tumour biology 🧬 and treatment modalities 💊 to symptom management, end-of-life care, and supporting families 👨‍👩‍👧 — this course spans the whole cancer journey, including its hardest chapters.

!!! quote "🕊️ Palliative philosophy"
    *"Cure sometimes, relieve often, comfort always."* — Palliative care adds life to days, not just days to life. 🌅

## 📋 Topics to Come

- 🧬 Cancer pathophysiology & staging (TNM)
- 💉 Chemotherapy, radiotherapy & immunotherapy safety
- 🤕 Symptom clusters: pain, nausea, fatigue
- 🗣️ Breaking bad news & advance care planning
- 🌅 End-of-life care & bereavement support
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Caregiver burden & family conferences

## 🧰 Comfort Toolkit (starter list)

- 💊 WHO analgesic ladder review
- 🌬️ Breathlessness management (fan, positioning, opioids)
- 🍽️ Cachexia & nutrition conversations
- 💬 SPIKES protocol for serious news

!!! tip "💡 Self-care reminder"
    Oncology placement can be emotionally heavy 🫂. Debrief with peers, use reflective practice, and look after *your* tank first. ⛽
